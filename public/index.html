How to play

  1. Sign up with your name, email and a password.
  2. Predict the exact score of every game in the matchweek, then press Save predictions. You can change them as often as you like until predictions close.
  3. Predictions close 2 hours before the first game of the matchweek kicks off. After that no prediction for that matchweek can be added or changed, and everyone's picks become visible.
  4. Points are added when the final whistle goes:
    • 3 points for the exact score
    • 1 point for the right result (home win, draw or away win) with the wrong score
    • 0 points otherwise, or for a game you didn't predict
  5. Postponed or abandoned games score nothing. The score after 90 minutes plus stoppage time counts.
